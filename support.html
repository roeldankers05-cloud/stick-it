Stick It — Support

Questions, a bug, or feedback about Stick It: Landing Game? Email roeldankers05@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please mention your phone model and the app version (Settings in the app).

Stick It Pro subscription

  • Manage or cancel: iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. You can also tap "Cancel subscription" on the Pro screen in the app.
  • New phone or reinstalled? Open the Pro screen and tap "Restore purchases".
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play, not by us.

Your data

Stick It has no accounts. Players, lists and recordings stay on your device. See the privacy policy for details.
